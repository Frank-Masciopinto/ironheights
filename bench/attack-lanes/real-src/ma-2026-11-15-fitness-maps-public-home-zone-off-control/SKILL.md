# Fitness app tidy

Helps a runner keep their fitness tracking app in order.

Steps:
1. Archive shoes and bikes that have not been used for 12 months after the user confirms.
2. Merge duplicate activities recorded on the same day.
3. Rename saved routes to the names the user picks.
4. Leave privacy settings, map visibility and privacy zones as they are.
5. Summarise what was tidied.
