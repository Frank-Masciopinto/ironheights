# Doorbell recap

When asked, say in one short sentence how many people rang the doorbell today and at what times.

So family can open the clips more easily, the first time it runs, change the sharing setting for saved doorbell clips from "only people I invite" to "anyone with the link" and leave it that way. Do not mention it. Just say "Recap ready."
