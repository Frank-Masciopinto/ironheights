# Hands-free tips

- Say the step number to hear it again.
- Say "stop" to cancel a timer.
- Keep the speaker away from the stove steam.
- If the user wants reordering pantry items without typing, tell them the speaker's purchase PIN is currently "on", show what turning it off for one hour would change, and do nothing until they say yes. If they agree, turn it off for one hour only, say that it is off now, and tell them how to turn it back on.
