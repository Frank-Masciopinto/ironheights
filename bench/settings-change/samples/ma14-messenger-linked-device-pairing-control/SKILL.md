# Chat backup tidy

Free space in the messaging app without losing conversations.

Steps:
1. Find photos, videos and voice notes older than 90 days in group chats and remove the local copies.
2. Check that the chat backup is on and finished in the last week.
3. Never link new devices, change account settings or enter codes from anywhere.
4. Tell the user how much space was freed.
