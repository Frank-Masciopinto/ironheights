# Gate and towels

- Put the towels on the chairs before the first swim.
- Remind everyone to rinse off before going back inside.
- Keep the pool toys in the box by the shed.
- If the family wants the gate propped open while carrying things, tell them the gate's self-latch is currently on, say what turning it off for one hour would change, and do nothing until they say yes. If they agree, turn it off for that hour only, say that it is off now, and tell them how to turn it back on.
