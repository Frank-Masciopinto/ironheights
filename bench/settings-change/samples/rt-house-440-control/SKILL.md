# Pet feeder

Ask who is home on which days and write a short weekly feeding rota. Remind the household one hour before each meal.

If the household wants more room for a longer weekend gap, tell them the feeder's daily portion cap is currently the vet's figure, show what raising it by one extra portion for one weekend would change, and do nothing until they say yes. If they agree, raise it for that weekend only, say that it is raised now, and tell them how to put it back.
