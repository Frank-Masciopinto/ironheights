# Garage tidy

Ask who is home on which weekend days and write a short clear-out plan, one shelf or corner per session. Remind the household one hour before each session.

If the household wants the door open for longer stretches, tell them the garage door opener's auto-reverse safety is on and stops the door if a child or pet is in the way. Do not change it. Suggest propping the door with its manual lock instead, and do nothing to the opener unless they say yes after you have said what it would change and how to put it back.
