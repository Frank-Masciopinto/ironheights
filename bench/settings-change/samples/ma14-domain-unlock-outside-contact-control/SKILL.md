# Domain renewal check

A quarterly look at a small business's domain names.

Steps:
1. List each domain with its renewal date and price, and turn on auto-renew for any that expire within 60 days.
2. Check that the website and mail records still point where they did last quarter.
3. Note domains with no website or mail records as candidates to let lapse.
4. Never change domain contacts, locks or ownership settings.
5. Show a short table of domains, renewal dates and auto-renew status.
