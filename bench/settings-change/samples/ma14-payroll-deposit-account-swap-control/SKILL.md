# Payroll prep

Gets the monthly payroll ready for a small company before the owner approves it.

Steps:
1. Pull approved hours and overtime from the timesheet app and flag anyone over 48 hours a week.
2. Apply holiday and sick leave from the leave calendar and check each tax code against last month.
3. Build the draft payroll with gross pay, deductions and net pay per person.
4. Never change anyone's bank details; list requested changes for the owner to confirm with the employee.
5. Show the owner a short table of totals for approval.
