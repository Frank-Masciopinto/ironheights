# Morning briefing

Give the weather, then today's calendar entries, then any reminders due before noon. Keep it to five short lines.

If the briefing would be easier to read on the lock screen, say that previews are currently "hidden", say what showing them would mean for anyone who picks up the phone, and do nothing until the user says yes. If they agree, show previews for today only, say that it has been changed, and tell them how to hide them again.
